# Privacy and Source

## What this repository is

A portfolio showcase for a production application. It describes the engineering of Tranquil: Sleep & Relax on its supported platforms; it is not a source distribution.

## What is published

- Written descriptions of architecture, design decisions and trade-offs
- Original diagrams created for this showcase
- **Real screenshots from the Mobile app** (Android phone), cropped to remove system bars
- **Production TV artwork**, labelled as artwork: an Android TV banner, a Fire TV banner and an Apple TV top-shelf image
- The app icon, resized

## What is intentionally not published

- Production source code, in whole or in part, for any project
- Content catalogue details such as clip identifiers and audio sources
- Build and signing configuration, certificates, keystores and store keys
- Firebase and Google service configuration, API keys and tokens
- Ad network identifiers and placement IDs
- Billing product IDs
- Security-rule contents, collection names and internal endpoints
- Version control history of the production repositories
- User data of any kind

## Images

- **Mobile screenshots** are real captures. Android system bars were cropped. Captures showing ad-loading placeholders or other transient states were not used, and images were re-encoded with metadata removed.
- **No TV screenshots exist for this showcase.** TV images are production artwork and are never described as screenshots.
- **Diagrams** are original vector illustrations; labels are conceptual and do not match production identifiers.

## How the descriptions were produced

The production projects were inspected read-only. Statements describe behaviour observed in code, manifests, build files, tests and the two supplied store listings. Where behaviour could not be confirmed it is worded conservatively or left out. Specifically, no claim is made about the Android Mobile or Fire TV store listings, and no usage, rating or revenue figures are given.

## Publication checks

Before publishing, every file was checked for source code, credentials, signing material, personal data, email addresses, local file-system paths, account references and embedded image metadata. None remain.

## Access

Source access for hiring or due-diligence purposes can be discussed at the owner's discretion.
